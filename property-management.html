Property Management

End-to-end management for Detroit-area property owners — so your investment runs smoothly whether you live down the street or across the country.

Our Approach

Management the Way You'd Do It Yourself — If You Had the Time.

Owning rental property should be a source of income, not a second job. 1701 Partners takes on the day-to-day so you can focus on your life, your work, or your next deal.

We treat every property like it's our own — because many of them are. We hold, manage, and operate our own portfolio alongside our clients', which means our incentives and our standards are aligned with yours.
