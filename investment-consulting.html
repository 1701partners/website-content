Investment Consulting

Local expertise, sharp underwriting, and real operator experience — for investors looking at the Detroit market.

What We Do

Guidance From People Who Own and Operate Here.

Real estate investing in Detroit isn't like anywhere else. Block-by-block pricing variation, nuanced tax foreclosure dynamics, evolving city policies, and a mix of housing stock from 1890s to today all mean the standard playbook doesn't apply.

We advise investors — from first-timers buying their first door to experienced operators scaling portfolios — on where to buy, what to pay, and how to execute. We're not selling a course or a fund: we're offering the same analysis we run on our own deals.

Consulting Engagements

How Investors Work With Us.

A

Market Strategy

Neighborhood-level analysis of rent growth, cap rates, turnover, and policy impacts — so you can pick your spots with confidence.

B

Deal Sourcing

Access to our pipeline of on- and off-market properties, plus help evaluating opportunities you bring to the table.

Underwriting Support

Conservative, Detroit-specific proformas that account for real-world capex, vacancy, and operating expenses — not rosy national averages.

Portfolio Review

An honest look at your existing portfolio: what's performing, what's not, and where there's room to optimize.

F

Exit Strategy

Guidance on disposition: when to sell, how to package, and how to maximize value at the end of a hold.
