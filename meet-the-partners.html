Meet the Partners

The people building, investing, and managing alongside you — rooted in Detroit, driven by results.

Our Team

Detroit-Native Leadership.

1701 Partners was founded on a simple idea: real estate done right is built on relationships, local expertise, and follow-through. James and Melissa bring complementary backgrounds in urban planning, marketing, and strategic business thinking — all focused on the Detroit market.

James

Founding Partner

Marketer by trade and Urban Planner by training, James knows what people want and what people deserve. Working toward equity has been James' goal throughout his career. His experience working at the City of Detroit and in his corporate roles, he has built programs that bring peoples needs into new projects and developments.

Working on the Michigan Central Station development for Ford Motor Company, James brought in diverse thinking and voices to place an equity lens on one of the largest corporate developments in Detroit's history. James brings this mentality to 1701 projects by listening to his potential tenants wants and ensuring they get the best options across the financial spectrum.

james@1701partners.com (248) 346-5688

Melissa

Founding Partner

Vibrant urban life is in Melissa's blood. Growing up in Southwest Detroit, Melissa learned early that a community is only as strong as the people in it. That is why she is dedicated to providing attainable, safe quality housing for families in Metro Detroit. By lifting up neighborhoods with new housing options she believes that we can lift up our communities, families and collective quality of life.

Melissa brings a business background focused on strategic thinking to 1701. An astute marketer and analyst, she understands what the market needs and can find the right house for the right family.

melissa@1701partners.com (734) 776-7226
Our Approach

Local Knowledge. Real Results.

James and Melissa have both walked the neighborhoods they invest in, pulled permits at the city, worked hands-on with residents, and led teams through complex development work. That experience informs how 1701 makes decisions — from which streets to buy on, to which contractors to trust, to which offers make sense for our investor partners.

We believe Detroit's best days are ahead, and we're proud to be part of building them.
