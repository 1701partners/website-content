Renovation Services

From Cosmetic Refresh to Full Gut Rehab.

Detroit is home to some of the most beautiful pre-war housing stock in the country — bungalows, Tudors, brick colonials, and classic two-family flats. But bringing these homes back requires more than cosmetic fixes. It requires a contractor who understands old bones, and an owner who knows what adds value versus what's just pretty.

1701 Partners manages renovations from simple turnovers to full-scope rehabs. We work on our own properties, investor-owned properties, and owner-occupied homes.

Scopes We Handle

Every Level of Renovation.

01

Turnover & Refresh

Between-tenant paint, flooring touch-ups, appliance swaps, and make-ready work to get units back on market fast.

02

Kitchen & Bath

Targeted renovations that drive the biggest rent bumps — new cabinets, counters, tile, fixtures, and finishes.

03

Full Gut Rehab

Down-to-studs renovations with new mechanicals, layouts, and finishes — ideal for distressed acquisitions.

04

Mechanical Updates

New roofs, HVAC, electrical service, plumbing stacks, and water heaters — the unsexy work that protects the asset.

05

Historic Restoration

Sensitive renovation of period features — original wood, plaster, leaded glass, tile — without sacrificing livability.

06

Multi-Unit Conversions

Reconfiguring two-family flats and small multi-unit buildings to modernize without compromising character.
